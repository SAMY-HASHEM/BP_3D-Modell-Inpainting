How it works

  1. Add data: pictures, a video, a 3D model, or any other file. Several pictures of the same thing are fine.
  2. Say what you want in your own words, for example “replace the toast in its hand with this book”.
  3. Check the plan: the page says which data it takes as the model and which as the new part. Change anything that is wrong.
  4. Press Run. Nothing starts before that. The city shows where the work is; you can stop it at any time.

Shortcuts: Enter send · Shift+Enter new line · Ctrl+V paste data · drop files anywhere · Esc close this

The city: the narrow left bank of the Nile is our device (your data comes in, your model comes back), the right bank is the uni server where all the work is done, the bridge is the cloud folder between them: click it to enter the Sciebo login and test the connection. A building that works glows; a finished one raises a green flag.

The bridge: Sciebo

The uni server can't be reached from home, but it and this computer both reach the Sciebo cloud: a folder there is the bridge. Your data goes over it to the uni server, and the results come back.

Enter the Sciebo login

In Sciebo: your picture (top right) › Persönliche Einstellungen › Sicherheit › Neues App-Passwort erstellen. Use the login name and the app password shown there, not your uni password.
